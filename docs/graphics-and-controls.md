# Graphics and controls

Find the setting you need, see its choices, and change one thing at a time. No heroic guesswork required.

## Open the menu

Press **F1** in game to open or close Longshot's menu. Choose the **Graphics** tab.
On a controller, use **Home** or **Select + Start**. The
[full menu reference](../README.md#in-game-menu) covers navigation and alternative shortcuts.

**Save before restarting.** Settings marked **After a restart** take effect on the next start.
Choosing **Restart now** in the game loses progress since your last save.

## Graphics settings

The website lists the choices and defaults documented for this version of Longshot.
Your saved settings may differ. Some options only appear with a supported GPU, driver or build;
HDR options also depend on the selected HDR mode. Expand a setting for its conditions.

Choices and defaults follow the maintained [menu reference](../README.md#in-game-menu).
Use it for detailed explanations and examples, including the sections referred to as “below” in the choices.

## Resolution and upscaling

The menu reference explains render resolution, output resolution, upscalers, window scaling, and sharpness.
Check it for the available modes and their hardware requirements before changing settings.

## Frame rate and frame generation

A game-frame cap and frame generation control different things. See the
[menu reference](../README.md#in-game-menu) for the current explanation and advice on higher frame caps.
**Caps above 60 FPS are experimental:** some bosses and cutscenes can misbehave. Generated frames do not increase
the game's own frame rate.
For reports about boss timing or HUD artifacts, follow the
[linked responses and trackers](common-issues.md).

## Comparing performance

Change one setting at a time and compare the same area. Follow the README's
[performance-reporting instructions](../README.md#low-fps-or-stutter) so the comparison includes useful log details.

## Controls

Use the [controls reference](../README.md#controls) for shortcuts and bindings. When reporting a mismatch, include
both Longshot's selected layout and the game's own control settings.
