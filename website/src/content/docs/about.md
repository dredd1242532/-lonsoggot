**Longshot by team magicbean.** Serious recompilation. Slightly emotional beans.

The site brings together player guides, development updates and support.
Meet [team magicbean](/Longshot/team/) and add your profile to the roster.
Follow the [team repository](https://git.magicbean.dev/MagicBeanDev/Longshot) for source, proposals and reports.

## Guides and updates

The player guides describe Longshot. The progress board shows a dated snapshot from the team's public Forgejo repository.
A merged change does not establish that it is included in a released build. Check release notes and the snapshot timestamp.
Search terms stay in your browser.

## Game files

We do not host, store or distribute game files, ROMs, game dumps or decryption keys.
Supply your own supported copy and build locally.

## Typography

Body text and controls use [Atkinson Hyperlegible Next](https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/), bundled locally under the
[SIL Open Font License](/Longshot/fonts/atkinson/OFL.txt). Libertinus Serif is retained for large headings; code uses monospace.
